## City prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed cinematic background for an ambient music web app. Create ONE exceptional horizontal 16:9 image, ideally 3840x2160. A quiet futuristic city seen from a high hillside or upper-story retreat across a rain-soaked river at blue-green dusk, realistic dense architectural layers and distant tiny warm amber windows, a few elegant subtly futuristic towers receding into mist, natural photographic lighting and atmospheric perspective. Mature fine-art cinema photography, analog 35mm texture very subtle, long exposure soft reflections, extraordinary depth and realistic materials, restrained desaturated petrol green, slate, dark forest shadows and warm accents. The image must feel like somewhere one could quietly stay, not a sci-fi game. Composition: left 40 percent and upper quarter are calm dark atmospheric negative space for pale app typography, main skyline detail concentrated middle-right around 50 to 75 percent of image height, foreground subtly dark and unfussy. Do not render any app interface. No giant monolith, no spaceship, no decorative vector geometry, no beam cones, no lurid neon, no magenta, no purple, no oversaturated cyberpunk, no text, no watermark, no characters. This is a complete beautiful photographic environment, not an SVG illustration or flat gradient. Landscape must remain legible and striking at ordinary screen brightness, avoid making everything nearly black. Thin rain and realistic distant haze, not sheets of white streaks. Render final background only.

## Afternoon prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed cinematic background for an ambient music web app, companion to a restrained dark atmospheric city photograph. Create ONE exceptional horizontal 16:9 image, ideally 3840x2160. A faded nostalgic late afternoon overlooking genuinely beautiful rolling countryside, delicate tall grasses in the foreground, overlapping soft hills and a distant stand of trees, golden sun diffused through light atmospheric haze near the upper right, muted sage green fields and warm dusty ochre light. Fine-art landscape photography on subtle analog 35mm film, tactile natural textures, extraordinary atmospheric depth, quiet and believable, deeply peaceful without looking like a stock wellness photograph. Composition: main scenic interest and gentle sunlight on the right, left 40 percent in soft darker olive shade with calm negative space for pale app typography, foreground base dark natural grasses and soft shadow, distant hills cross the lower-middle part of frame. Slightly wistful color, realistic atmospheric light, nuanced tones and gentle photographic softness, not blurred-out shapes. Avoid bright pure white sky, exaggerated orange sunsets, oversaturated green, fantasy scenery, vector hills, cartoon trees, geometric forms, signs, buildings as focal objects, people, text, logos, watermarks, frames or UI. Render final background only. It should pair naturally with an evening city image in one calm ambient instrument.
